Chapter 2: Respiration

100 Comprehensive Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)
​
1. Respiration is primarily which type of process?
  • A) Anabolism
  • B) Catabolism
  • C) Synthesis
  • D) Photosynthesis
Correct Answer: B) Catabolism
​
2. What is the primary simple food molecule that breaks down during respiration to release energy?
  • A) Amino acid
  • B) Fatty acid
  • C) Glucose
  • D) Glycerol
Correct Answer: C) Glucose
​
3. What is the energy currency of the cell produced during respiration?
  • A) DNA
  • B) RNA
  • C) ATP
  • D) NADP
Correct Answer: C) ATP
​
4. Respiration that takes place in the presence of oxygen is called:
  • A) Anaerobic respiration
  • B) Aerobic respiration
  • C) Fermentation
  • D) Glycolysis
Correct Answer: B) Aerobic respiration
​
5. Respiration that takes place in the absence of oxygen is called:
  • A) Aerobic respiration
  • B) Anaerobic respiration
  • C) Biological oxidation
  • D) Photophosphorylation
Correct Answer: B) Anaerobic respiration
​
6. How many phases of cellular respiration are there?
  • A) 2
  • B) 3
  • C) 4
  • D) 5
Correct Answer: B) 3
​
7. Which of the following is NOT a phase of cellular respiration?
  • A) Glycolysis
  • B) Citric acid cycle
  • C) Calvin cycle
  • D) Electron transport system
Correct Answer: C) Calvin cycle
​
8. Where does the glycolysis process take place inside the cell?
  • A) Mitochondria matrix
  • B) Cytoplasm (Cytosol)
  • C) Nucleus
  • D) Ribosome
Correct Answer: B) Cytoplasm (Cytosol)
​
9. Where does the citric acid cycle take place?
  • A) Cytoplasm
  • B) Plasma membrane
  • C) Mitochondrial matrix
  • D) Ribosome
Correct Answer: C) Mitochondrial matrix
​
10. Where is the electron transport system located?
  • A) Outer mitochondrial membrane
  • B) Inner mitochondrial membrane
  • C) Cytoplasm
  • D) Nucleoplasm
Correct Answer: B) Inner mitochondrial membrane
​
11. What is the chemical formula of glucose?
  • A) C_6H_{12}O_6
  • B) C_{12}H_{22}O_{11}
  • C) CH_3COOH
  • D) C_2H_5OH
Correct Answer: A) C_6H_{12}O_6
​
12. How many carbon atoms are present in a glucose molecule?
  • A) 3
  • B) 4
  • C) 5
  • D) 6
Correct Answer: D) 6

13. In the first step of glycolysis, a 6-carbon glucose molecule breaks down into how many 3-carbon simple sugars?
  • A) 1
  • B) 2
  • C) 3
  • D) 4
Correct Answer: B) 2
​
14. What is the end product of glycolysis formed from 3-carbon sugars?
  • A) Lactic acid
  • B) Ethanol
  • C) Pyruvic acid
  • D) Citric acid
Correct Answer: C) Pyruvic acid
​
15. Pyruvic acid is a carbon compound containing how many carbon atoms?
  • A) 2
  • B) 3
  • C) 4
  • D) 6
Correct Answer: B) 3
​
16. What does NADH2 stand for?
  • A) Reduced Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide
  • B) Reduced Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide Phosphate
  • C) Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide Hydride
  • D) Nicotinamide Adenine Diphosphate
Correct Answer: A) Reduced Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide
​
17. What does FADH2 stand for?
  • A) Flavin Adenine Dinucleotide Hydride
  • B) Reduced Flavin Adenine Dinucleotide
  • C) Formyl Adenine Dinucleotide Hydrogen
  • D) Flavin Adenine Diphosphate
Correct Answer: B) Reduced Flavin Adenine Dinucleotide
​
18. In yeast cells under total absence of oxygen, pyruvic acid is converted into:
  • A) Lactic acid
  • B) Ethanol (Alcohol)
  • C) Citric acid
  • D) Oxaloacetic acid
Correct Answer: B) Ethanol (Alcohol)
​
19. Alcoholic fermentation in yeast produces which 2-carbon compound?
  • A) Acetic acid
  • B) Ethanol
  • C) Methanol
  • D) Propanol
Correct Answer: B) Ethanol

20. Under oxygen deficiency (lack of oxygen) in muscle cells, pyruvic acid is converted into:
  • A) Ethanol
  • B) Lactic acid
  • C) Acetic acid
  • D) Carbon dioxide and water
Correct Answer: B) Lactic acid
​
21. Accumulation of which acid in muscle cells causes muscle cramps during heavy exercise?
  • A) Citric acid
  • B) Pyruvic acid
  • C) Lactic acid

Correct Answer: C) Lactic acid

  • A) Pyruvic acid
